# Everything Soil — Project Specification

## 1. Project Identity

**Organization:** Everything Soil  
**Website:** https://everythingsoil.com  
**Core physical location:** Everything Soil Farm / Center  
**Primary concept:** Panja Bootham (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space)

Everything Soil is an organization, knowledge platform, community and physical nature/meditation center built around understanding and experiencing the five elements of nature.

The physical center is intended as a place for meditation, yoga, nature experience, soil awareness, contemplation, community activities and an experiential understanding of Panja Bootha Lingeswarar.

It is **not intended to operate as a conventional temple**.

## 2. Core Philosophy

Everything begins with nature and the five elements.

Soil is the foundation of life. Earth, water, fire, air and space are not merely abstract concepts; they can be observed through nature, the human body, breath, food, agriculture, environment and lived experience.

The organization should invite people to observe, experience and reflect rather than require them to accept a particular belief.

Core journey:

**Discover → Learn → Experience → Participate → Belong → Contribute**

## 3. Vision

Create a living ecosystem where people can reconnect with soil, nature, the five elements and their own inner awareness.

## 4. Mission

- Educate people about soil and the natural world.
- Introduce the five elements through accessible educational and experiential content.
- Provide spaces for meditation, yoga and contemplation.
- Encourage natural farming and ecological stewardship.
- Build a community of volunteers, practitioners, learners and supporters.
- Develop projects that restore soil, water, biodiversity and natural spaces.
- Create meaningful opportunities for people to visit and experience the physical center.

## 6. Organization Vocabulary

Preferred terminology:

- **Center** rather than Temple when referring to the physical facility.
- **Experience** rather than Event when describing a recurring type of activity.
- **Circle** rather than Group for community groups.
- **Contribution** rather than Donation where context allows.
- **Journey** rather than Progress for a person's personal development.
- **Facilitator** rather than Teacher where appropriate.
- **Member** for people who intentionally join the community.
- **Volunteer** for people contributing time or skills.

## 7. Audience

The platform should welcome:

- Nature lovers
- Farmers
- Natural farming practitioners
- Meditation practitioners
- Yoga practitioners
- Spiritual seekers
- People interested in traditional knowledge
- Environmentalists
- Students
- Families
- Researchers
- Volunteers
- People simply curious about soil and nature

Avoid making the platform dependent on a single religious, spiritual or ideological viewpoint.

## 8. Languages

Primary languages:

- English
- Tamil

The architecture must support multilingual content from the beginning.

## 9. Technology

Backend:
- Django

Frontend:

- Bootstrap 5
- HTMX where useful

Authentication:
- Google OAuth / Google Sign-In

Development philosophy:
- Modular Django monolith
- Mobile-first
- SEO-friendly
- Accessible
- Secure
- Maintainable

## 10. Product Principle

The website should not primarily optimize for donations.

The preferred journey is:

**Curiosity → Learning → Experience → Community → Participation → Contribution**

The physical center is the heart of the organization; the website is the gateway; the community is the living body.
