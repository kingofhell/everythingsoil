Soil Biology

A single teaspoon of healthy soil contains more living organisms than there are people on Earth.

💧

Water Security

Organic rich soil holds water like a sponge, mitigating both droughts and local floods naturally.

🍂

Carbon Sequestration

Soil holds three times more carbon than the atmosphere, serving as an essential ecological carbon sink.
